# Awesome Fable Game

An index of playable games circulating in the community that are said to have been made with help from Claude Fable 5.2.

A short index of browser-playable games that people in the community say were made with help from Claude Fable 5.2. This list does not independently verify the model, the toolchain, or any release timeline.

This repository is an index only. Games and their assets belong to their original authors. Being listed here does not mean licensing or authorization has been reviewed, and it does not mean we have played or endorsed the games. To play, go to the links published by the authors, and respect their licenses and wishes.

## Games

- **[Starry Nightrider](https://starrynightrider.com/)** — Ride a bicycle through van Gogh's *The Starry Night*, collecting orbs and delivering them to the church steeple. Author [@ebbyamir](https://x.com/ebbyamir) · [Demo post](https://x.com/ebbyamir/status/2120284261089673376)
- **[Saltline](https://claude.ai/artifact/BGY1SztrAYcJQHSsnHHGmp)** — A first-person shooter set on a rainy-night dock; according to the author, the models, textures, and sound effects were all generated in code. Author [@felixrieseberg](https://x.com/felixrieseberg) · [Demo post](https://x.com/felixrieseberg/status/2119925633564463609)
- **[What the Water Keeps](https://what-the-water-keeps.vercel.app/)** — A slow boat ride by lantern light through several dreamlike worlds, with nothing to win or lose. Author [@kevin_t_ngo](https://x.com/kevin_t_ngo) · [Demo post](https://x.com/kevin_t_ngo/status/2119868968307695818)
- **[MIRROR EARTH](https://www.spawn.co/@mattshumer/earth?room=local)** — A voxel sandbox set on Earth, where you can roam and destroy things; the author says the weather corresponds to the real Earth. Author [@mattshumer_](https://x.com/mattshumer_) · [Demo post](https://x.com/mattshumer_/status/2119877065701036047)
- **[Portals](https://portals-endless-worlds.netlify.app/)** — An endless roaming journey through 3D worlds linked by portals. According to the author, a large number of agents generated hundreds of worlds inspired by books, art, and places. Author [@emollick](https://x.com/emollick) · [Demo post](https://x.com/emollick/status/2119918307457577163)
- **[Evensong](https://evensong-phi.vercel.app/)** — A souls-like boss-fight demo set in a drowned cathedral. Author [@kevin_t_ngo](https://x.com/kevin_t_ngo) · [Demo post](https://x.com/kevin_t_ngo/status/2120238844754923874)
- **[Siege Wing](https://bannerfall.itch.io/siege-wing)** — Pilot an attack helicopter and assault a medieval castle and its army within a time limit. Author [@builderjaydub](https://x.com/builderjaydub) · [Demo post](https://x.com/builderjaydub/status/2120223731520040966)
- **[I Would Have Gotten Away With It](https://i-would-have-gotten-away-with-it.netlify.app/)** — A reverse Scooby-Doo: you play as the monster, scaring off meddling kids. Author [@emollick](https://x.com/emollick) · [Demo post](https://x.com/emollick/status/2119876449494540554)
- **[Gilt & Gutter](https://gilt-and-gutter.vercel.app/)** — A 1v1 first-person duel where shots come in gold or teal. Author [@kevin_t_ngo](https://x.com/kevin_t_ngo) · [Demo post](https://x.com/kevin_t_ngo/status/2119900487688622171)
- **[Hogwarts Royale](https://www.spawn.co/@mattshumer/hogwarts-royale)** — An unofficial fan-made battle royale in the style of Harry Potter and Fortnite, fought on broomsticks. Author [@mattshumer_](https://x.com/mattshumer_) · [Demo post](https://x.com/mattshumer_/status/2119872098306810226)
- **[Dread & District](https://dread-and-district.netlify.app/)** — A transit-dispatch sim in a fantasy world: connect settlements and fight chaos with logistics. Author [@emollick](https://x.com/emollick) · [Demo post](https://x.com/emollick/status/2120248525254742318)
- **[Turbo Tails](https://turbo-tails.vercel.app/)** — A 3D kart racer you can play in the browser, with multiple tracks and the option to race with friends. Author [@petergyang](https://x.com/petergyang) · [Demo post](https://x.com/petergyang/status/2119901658394358005)
- **[Banana Mine Rush](https://remix.gg/g/banana-mine-rush)** — A mine-cart racer on the Remix platform; open it in the browser and play. Author [@0xChuckstock](https://x.com/0xChuckstock) · [Demo post](https://x.com/0xChuckstock/status/2120125184606658728)

## Collections and discovery

- **[DSH Game Store](https://dsh-game-store.com/)** — A web store and discovery platform for "games people made with agents." Games in the catalog can be opened and played in the browser, and you can also submit titles for listing by following the site's instructions. It is listed here as a collection entry, not as a single game.

## Listing guidelines

New entries are welcome. Please try to meet the following:

1. Include a playable link that opens directly (do not list video-only work).
2. Credit the author and link to their public homepage or account.
3. Link to the original demo post from the author or another reliable public source.
4. Describe the genre in one sentence; do not include unverified popularity numbers, and do not state unverified model identity or release dates as fact.
5. Collections, platforms, or stores should go under "Collections and discovery," not be listed as a single game.

New entries can follow this format:

```markdown
- **[Game title](playable link)** — One-sentence genre description. Author [@handle](https://x.com/handle) · [Demo post](https://x.com/handle/status/...)
```
